## Key Tips for Sending These Emails:

✅ **Personalization is CRITICAL**
- Use the person's actual name (not "Hi there" or "Hi [Name]")
- Reference a specific article or resource they created
- Show you know what they do

✅ **Keep it SHORT**
- No more than 3-4 short paragraphs
- Get to the point in the first sentence
- Make it easy to say yes

✅ **Focus on THEIR audience first**
- Don't lead with "promote me"
- Lead with "this helps your readers"
- Show genuine value alignment

✅ **Include a CTA (Call-to-Action)**
- Ask for something specific (link, guest post, feature)
- Make it easy to respond

✅ **Follow-up matters**
- If no response in 5-7 days, send ONE polite follow-up
- After that, move on to the next person
